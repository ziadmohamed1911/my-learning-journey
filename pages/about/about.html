Hi there! My name is Ziad and welcome to my learning journal.

After several months of learning frontend development on my own, I’ve made the big jump into full-stack, working toward a career change from customer service to building things people use every day.

How I stay committed to learning

I like to think of myself as a lifelong learner. After four years in customer service, I decided to change careers and started learning frontend development, aiming to become a full-stack developer. I spend time learning every day, then build small projects from what I learned or add new techniques to projects I’ve already made.

While that was fun, I wanted to share what I was learning and the story of my journey with the world.

How I got started

I started simple and gradually grew my learning journal site. I take notes while I learn, and after each session I use them to reflect and write short summaries in my own words.

That helps me truly understand what I’m learning, and I realized that posting my summaries could also help others learn and stay motivated.
